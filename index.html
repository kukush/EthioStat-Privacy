EthioStat is an offline-first finance and telecom balance tracker for Ethiopia. This policy explains what the app accesses, why it needs SMS permissions, and how your data stays under your control.

Effective September 17, 2026Last updated September 17, 2026
100% offline processing

EthioStat does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your financial or telecom data with third parties.

Your SMS is read locally to build your transaction and balance history.

Overview

ETHIOSTAT is a professional Android utility designed to bring digital clarity to the often confusing mobile experience in Ethiopia by transforming complex, "pseudo-code" telecom and financial SMS notifications into a clean, visual dashboard. The app solves the real-world problem of fragmented and difficult-to-read alerts by automatically extracting and summarising essential data points.The App is developed and maintained by Qello Nexus Team.

All data processing occurs entirely on your device. EthioStat has no account system, backend, analytics, advertising SDK, or cloud synchronization.

Information we access

With your permission, EthioStat reads SMS messages, sender addresses, and timestamps from banks, Telebirr, and Ethio Telecom. It uses this information to parse transactions, balances, packages, and related history. You choose which transaction sources are enabled.

Data Purpose Storage Shared
SMS messages Parse financial transaction and balance details. On-device Room database Never
Sender address Identify the bank or telecom provider. On-device Room database Never
SMS timestamp Associate records with the correct date and time. On-device Room database Never

Information we do not access

  • Contacts: EthioStat does not read your contacts.
  • Location: EthioStat does not access GPS or network location.
  • Camera and microphone: EthioStat does not access either.
  • Phone calls: EthioStat does not make or intercept calls.
  • Internet: EthioStat does not make API, analytics, or advertising requests.
  • Device identifiers: EthioStat does not read IMEI, Android ID, or advertising ID.
  • Other apps: EthioStat does not access data from other applications.

Storage and security

Records are stored locally in the Android application sandbox using Room and DataStore. PIN values are encrypted with AES-GCM using an Android Keystore key. The app has no remote storage or synchronization.

Sharing and retention

EthioStat does not transmit or share personal or financial data. Data remains on your device until you delete records in the app, clear app data in Android Settings, or uninstall EthioStat. Android then removes the application data.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke SMS and notification permissions at any time in Android Settings. Without SMS permission, historical scanning and automatic SMS transaction detection are unavailable, while local source configuration remains available.

Permissions explained

Permission Why it is needed Without it
READ_SMS Read existing bank and Telebirr SMS messages. Historical SMS scanning is unavailable.
RECEIVE_SMS Detect new incoming financial SMS messages. New transactions are not detected automatically.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show sync and foreground-service status. Background sync status notifications are unavailable.

Children's privacy

EthioStat is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
